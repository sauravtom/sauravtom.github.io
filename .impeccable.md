## Design Context

### Users
Indian developers, from students to working engineers, who want a practical path for understanding and creating agent skills. They should be able to scan the page, follow a complete example, and leave with a skill they can build and test in Codex.

### Brand Personality
Practical, grounded, and encouraging. The voice explains technical ideas without ceremony, distinguishes official facts from community advice, and feels like a thoughtful senior engineer sharing a field guide.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial workshop manual with Indian print cues: warm paper, strong ink, vermilion and green accents, ruled diagrams, and expressive serif/devanagari typography. Light-first, responsive, accessible, and intentionally distinct from generic dark developer dashboards or neon AI gradients.

### Design Principles

1. Teach through a visible learning trail: mental model, anatomy, build, test, and field notes.
2. Keep English and Hindi equally usable, not merely translated labels.
3. Label facts, examples, and practical advice so readers know what is official and what is editorial guidance.
4. Prefer runnable examples, checklists, and copyable snippets over abstract explanation.
5. Build for narrow mobile screens, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and strong color contrast from the start.
